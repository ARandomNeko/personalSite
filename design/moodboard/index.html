Cloud
chamber

I built a cloud chamber to make particle tracks visible. It is a striking kind of instrument: a particle too small to see can leave a trail large enough to observe with your eyes.

A window to read.

Compact monospace navigation. Thin, hard window borders. Serif titles and readable body text inside a dark document pane.

A faint four-tone texture stays at the outer edges of the original brushwork. The treatment is part of the composition, with no switches for the reader.
